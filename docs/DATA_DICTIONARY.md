# Workbook data dictionary

This document describes the tabs and populated-row counts in the exported workbook **EDGE GLASS - SOCIAL INTELLIGENCE DATABASE.xlsx** supplied for this project review.

## Snapshot summary

Counts below are populated data rows, excluding the header row. They describe this export only; they are not live counts.

| Sheet | Populated data rows | Purpose / observed contents |
|---|---:|---|
| Trend Research | 92 | Collected research records and AI analysis fields |
| Intelligence History | 92 | Historical analysis records and change-status fields |
| Daily Reports | 11 | Generated daily summaries and strategy/action sections |
| Content Ideas | 10 | Broader platform content plans |
| Instagram Content Ideas | 15 | Detailed Instagram Reel concepts and supporting research references |
| Competitor Research | 0 | Empty tab in this workbook snapshot |
| Audience Insights | 0 | Empty tab in this workbook snapshot |

## Sheet details

### Trend Research

Observed columns:

- Date
- Platform
- Topic
- Source URL
- Popularity Signal
- Relevance
- Opportunity
- AI Relevance Score
- Target Audience
- Content Opportunity
- Recommended Platform
- Content Format
- Priority
- AI Reasoning
- Analysis Status

This is the main research output table. It combines source metadata/context with AI-generated relevance, audience, opportunity and recommendation fields.

### Intelligence History

Observed columns:

- Analysis Date
- Discovery Date
- Source URL
- Topic
- Relevance Score
- Target Audience
- Content Opportunity
- Recommended Platform
- Content Format
- Priority
- AI Reasoning
- Change Status

This tab stores historical analysis records and a change-status field. In the supplied export it has 92 populated data rows, although the worksheet has 999 formatted/available rows.

### Daily Reports

Observed columns:

- Report Date
- Records Analysed
- Executive Summary
- Top Opportunities
- Audience Insights
- Platform Strategy
- Priority Action Plan

The 11 populated rows contain report-level summaries and recommendations. The report fields include platform strategy and a prioritised action plan.

### Content Ideas

- Platform

- Content Hook

- Caption

- Hashtags

The 10 populated rows contain broader content plans across platforms, including hooks, outlines, captions, creative direction and status/performance fields.

### Instagram Content Ideas

Observed columns:

- Topic

- Reel Concept
- Content Angle
- Opening Hook
- Story Summary
- Suggested Scenes

- Hashtags

The 15 populated rows contain Instagram Reel concepts with story and production guidance, plus YouTube research references.

### Competitor Research

This tab is present but empty in the supplied export. Do not describe competitor monitoring or competitor analysis as an operational feature based on this workbook.

### Audience Insights

This tab is present but empty in the supplied export. The `Daily Reports` sheet does contain a report field called `Audience Insights`, but the dedicated `Audience Insights` tab itself has no populated records in this snapshot. Do not conflate the two.

## Interpretation notes

- A populated sheet or column is evidence that data exists in this export; it does not by itself prove that every field is automatically refreshed or validated.
- AI-generated claims, engagement interpretations and proposed performance metrics should be checked before being used externally.
- The workbook includes both historical records and generated ideas. They are outputs of different stages and should not be counted as unique published social posts.
